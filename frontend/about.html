ABOUT CRESENT

Learning with purpose, character and confidence.

CRESENT High School gives students a disciplined, supportive environment where academic excellence and personal growth develop side by side.

A school built around the whole student

Our work is grounded in clear expectations, committed teaching and a belief that every learner deserves the opportunity to discover their strengths.

Our mission

To nurture knowledgeable, disciplined and compassionate young people prepared to contribute positively to their communities.

Our values

Excellence, discipline, integrity, respect and service guide the way we teach, learn and work together.

Our community

Students, families, teachers and staff share responsibility for creating a safe and ambitious school community.
