Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password, click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

How do I submit an assignment?

Go to the Assignments page, find your assignment, click on it, and use the "Submit Assignment" button. Upload your file and add any comments before submitting.

How do I view my grades?

Your grades are available on the Transcript page. You can view detailed grade breakdowns and academic performance over time.

How do I make a payment?

Visit the Finance page and navigate to the Payments section. You can make payments using M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer options available.

How do I contact my lecturer?

Use the Lecturer Interaction page to send messages, schedule meetings, or join live chat sessions with your lecturers.

What should I do if I encounter a technical issue?

Try refreshing the page first. If the issue persists, contact our technical support team using the contact options above or visit the Technical Support section.
